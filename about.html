A small team, one standard.

NOVA Home Care is a small, local cleaning team serving Cotonou and nearby areas. We built our process around one idea: you shouldn't have to wonder what quality you'll get this time versus last time. Every clean follows the same checklist, and every checklist is checked before we call the job done.

Why the checklist exists

Cleaning quality usually depends on who shows up that day. We wanted to remove that variable — so we built a standard checklist for every service, and a second check before any job is marked finished.

Quality-control process

Every visit is checked against the same checklist before it's called done — not by the person who cleaned, but as a separate step.

Local service

Based in Cotonou. We currently serve Akpakpa, Cadjehoun, Fidjrossè, Ganhi, Gbegamey, Haie Vive, Jonquet, Missèbo, and Zogbo, plus neighboring Abomey-Calavi and Godomey. Not sure if we cover your area? Enter it in the quote form and we'll confirm.
